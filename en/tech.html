Our targets

Three targets and their potential applications.

Each target has its own biology and its own medical need. Our agent builds a pH-dependent Smart Binder for each one.

01

Metabolic

GLP-1R

Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor

A receptor that helps regulate appetite, blood sugar and metabolism. Drugs that target it reshaped the treatment of obesity and type 2 diabetes.

Potential applications

  • Obesity and weight management
  • Type 2 diabetes
  • Cardiometabolic health

Our approach

A pH-dependent Smart Binder, for sharper activation and lower toxicity.

View the program

02

Muscle

Myostatin / GDF-8

Growth differentiation factor 8

A protein that holds back muscle growth. Blocking it could help preserve, even build, muscle mass.

Potential applications

  • Preserving muscle during weight loss
  • Age-related sarcopenia and frailty
  • Muscle diseases and cachexia

Our approach

A Smart Binder designed for sharper precision and lower toxicity.

03

Brain and oncology

TfR1 / CD71

Transferrin receptor 1

An iron receptor, abundant on the barrier that protects the brain and on fast-dividing cells. It can serve as an entry point to carry biologic drugs into the brain or to those cells.

Potential applications

  • Carrying biologic drugs into the brain
  • Neurological diseases
  • Cancers that overexpress TfR1

Our approach

A pH-dependent Smart Binder, designed to bind at the surface then release inside the cell.
