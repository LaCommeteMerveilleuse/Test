Smart Binders

A new class of medicine.

NovaDiff active builds Smart Binders. They are pH-dependent proteins that change conformation to reduce toxicity.

pH-dependent

Switches on where the local pH calls for it.

Changes conformation

It refolds with its surroundings to act in the right place.

Less toxic

Designed from the start for a lower toxicity profile.
